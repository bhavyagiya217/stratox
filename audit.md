# Stratox Audit Log & Compliance Check

## Overview
This document outlines the infrastructure compliance and FinOps audit benchmarks evaluated by Stratox.

## Audit Pillars
- **IaC Configuration Standards:** Pre-deployment validation against overprovisioning.
- **Zombie Resource Tracking:** Continuous sweeps for orphaned EBS volumes, unattached EIPs, and idle load balancers.
- **Multi-Cloud Pricing Discrepancies:** Cost efficiency indexing between AWS, GCP, and Azure.
- **Security & IAM Guardrails:** Least-privilege IAM audits and network perimeter exposure scans.
